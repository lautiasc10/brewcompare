Our take

What it is actually like

The Dinamica Plus is what happens when a manufacturer stops chasing a single headline feature and just builds the whole machine properly. The grinder is a thirteen-step steel conical burr, the same adjustment range as the much cheaper Magnifica Evo, but here it feeds a 19-bar system and a 3.5-inch colour touchscreen with sixteen programmed recipes including iced coffee. Nothing about it is class-leading in isolation. Together it is the most complete machine under $1,000 in this catalogue.

The LatteCrema carafe is the detail that changes daily life. It froths automatically for one-touch cappuccinos, then comes off and goes in the fridge with the milk still in it — which means you are not pouring milk away or washing a carafe after every session. It runs its own cleaning cycle and the parts are dishwasher safe. The brew group is removable, so you keep the ability to actually inspect the machine.

What you are paying the premium for over a Philips 5400 is the extraction and the interface, not the milk. What you give up against a Jura is the last increment of espresso quality and a sense of solidity — this is a plastic machine in a titanium finish, and it feels like one. The Bluetooth app is the weakest part and owners say so: connectivity is slow and the interface is clunky. You never need it.

What works

  • 19-bar extraction with a 13-step steel conical burr grinder
  • 16 one-touch recipes including a proper iced coffee program
  • 3.5-inch colour touchscreen — the clearest interface under $1,000 here
  • LatteCrema carafe detaches and goes straight into the fridge
  • Removable brew group and dishwasher-safe milk parts
  • Rated 4.5/5 across 232 reviews, with 89% recommending it

What does not

  • The Coffee Link app is slow and clunky, by consistent owner report
  • 1.8 L tank needs frequent refilling for a busy household
  • Plastic housing at a price where some rivals feel more solid
  • No published noise figure
  • User profile support is unclear in De'Longhi's own documentation

Ideal for

Someone buying one machine for a household of two to four who wants espresso quality and one-touch milk without stepping up to Jura money.

Probably not for

A buyer who wants the app to be a real part of the experience, or who needs a tank that survives a full day of guests.
