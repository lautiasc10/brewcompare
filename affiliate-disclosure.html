Affiliate disclosure

Required by Amazon's operating agreement, and by basic honesty. Here is exactly how this site makes money.

The short version

BrewCompare earns money through the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program. When you click a “View on Amazon” link on this site and buy something, Amazon pays us a commission. You pay the same price you would have paid anyway.

The required statement

BrewCompare is a participant in the Amazon Services LLC Associates Program, an affiliate advertising program designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by advertising and linking to Amazon.com.

As an Amazon Associate we earn from qualifying purchases. Amazon, the Amazon logo, AmazonSupply and the AmazonSupply logo are trademarks of Amazon.com, Inc. or its affiliates.

What that does and does not affect

  • It does not change the price you pay. Not by a cent.
  • It does not determine our scores. Our scoring rules are published in full on the methodology page and applied identically to every machine.
  • It does not buy placement. No manufacturer can pay to appear, to rank higher, or to have a criticism removed.
  • It does mean we have a commercial interest in you buying something. You should read our recommendations knowing that, and we would rather say so plainly than bury it.

About the prices on this site

Prices shown are a snapshot from the date printed next to them. Amazon prices change daily, vary by seller and by product variant, and can differ from what you see when you click through. The price on Amazon at the moment of purchase is the only one that counts. We label every price with its capture date rather than pretending it is live.

About the product information

Specifications come from manufacturers and from published reviews, and the sources are linked at the bottom of every machine page. Where a figure is not published we show a dash instead of guessing. Product illustrations on this site are our own drawings and represent layout, not exact appearance.
