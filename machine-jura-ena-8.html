Our take

What it is actually like

The ENA 8 exists for one reason: counter space. Jura took the milk technology from the E8 and S8 — the HP3 fine-foam frother, which is the genuinely good part of a Jura — and put it in the smallest chassis they build. Fifteen one-touch specialities behind a 2.8-inch colour display, in a machine that fits where an E8 will not.

The capacity is where that shows. The tank holds 1.1 litres and the hopper 125 grams, roughly half the E8 on both counts. For one or two people making two or three drinks a day that is a minor daily chore. For a household of four it is a refill every morning and again after lunch, and at that point you have bought the wrong machine.

The other omission is Pulse Extraction. On the E8 the machine pulses water through the puck in bursts, which measurably improves ristretto and espresso — it is the reason the E8 makes the best cup in this catalogue. The ENA 8 brews conventionally. It still makes very good coffee through the Aroma G3 grinder, better than anything under $1,000 here, but the specific thing you are paying Jura money for is not in this model. And like every Jura, the brew unit is sealed: cleaning is chemical, on Jura's schedule, with Jura's tablets.

What works

  • HP3 fine-foam milk system, the same one used on the E8 and S8
  • The smallest footprint of any premium machine in this catalogue
  • 15 one-touch specialities on a 2.8-inch colour display
  • Aroma G3 steel conical burr grinder
  • Fully automatic rinse and milk-cleaning programs

What does not

  • 1.1 L tank and 125 g hopper — roughly half the E8, and the smallest here
  • No Pulse Extraction Process, the E8's main advantage
  • Sealed brew unit: no manual cleaning, and you are committed to Jura consumables
  • Jura publishes no grind-setting count for this model
  • Costs more than the far more capable Dinamica Plus

Ideal for

One or two people who want Jura milk quality and genuinely cannot fit a full-size machine on the counter.

Probably not for

A household of three or more — you would spend the difference refilling it. If space allows, the E8 is the better machine and the Dinamica Plus is the better value.
