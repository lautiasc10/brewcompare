Our take

What it is actually like

Everything Philips learned about making milk drinks survivable in a normal kitchen is in this machine, and almost nothing else is. The LatteGo carafe is identical in concept to the one on the 5400: two pieces, no tubes, rinse it at the tap. At this price that is genuinely unusual — most machines under $600 either give you a manual wand or a milk system with tubing you will stop cleaning within a month.

The compromise is in the cup. The brew basket has relatively large holes and the extraction runs fast, which produces a lighter, thinner espresso than the De'Longhi Magnifica Evo at a similar price. If you drink milk-based coffee, the milk covers most of that. If you drink straight espresso, you will notice, and the Magnifica Evo is the better buy despite its manual wand.

Five one-touch drinks is a short menu, but they are the five most people actually make. The ceramic burrs give twelve grind steps and Philips rates them for 20,000 cups. AquaClean stretches descaling to a claimed 5,000 cups with timely filter changes, which is the difference between a machine you maintain occasionally and one that nags. It is noisy — reviewers consistently flag the grinder and frother — and the shell is unashamedly plastic.

What works

  • Tube-free LatteGo carafe at the lowest price in this catalogue
  • 100% ceramic burrs with 12 grind steps, rated for 20,000 cups
  • Removable brew group you can rinse under the tap
  • AquaClean stretches descaling to a claimed 5,000 cups
  • Compact enough for a small kitchen at 24.6 cm wide

What does not

  • Espresso is light and fast — the weakest shot in this catalogue
  • Only 5 one-touch drinks and a single user profile
  • Noisy grinder and frother, and no published decibel figure
  • Plainly plastic build
  • No iced coffee program

Ideal for

Someone who drinks cappuccinos and lattes rather than espresso, wants one-touch milk, and has a hard ceiling around $550.

Probably not for

An espresso drinker — the shot is the weakest here, and the Magnifica Evo beats it in the cup for similar money.
