Privacy and cookies

A static site with no accounts and no tracking. Here is what that means in practice.

The short version

This site is a set of static pages. It has no accounts, no login, no comment system and no server-side database. We do not ask you for personal information and we have nowhere to store it if you gave it to us.

Cookies

We do not set any tracking cookies ourselves. Your machine selections in the comparator are held in the page URL, not in a cookie, which is why you can copy a comparison link and send it to someone.

Third parties

  • Amazon. When you click a “View on Amazon” link you leave this site and Amazon's own privacy policy and cookies apply. Amazon uses a cookie to attribute any purchase to us so it can pay the commission described in our affiliate disclosure.
  • Google Fonts. The site loads two typefaces from Google's font service, which means your browser makes a request to Google when the page loads.
  • GitHub Pages. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps standard server logs.

Analytics

No analytics scripts are installed at present. If that changes, this page will say so before it happens rather than after.

Your rights

Since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to export, correct or delete. For data Amazon or Google hold, contact them directly under their own policies.
